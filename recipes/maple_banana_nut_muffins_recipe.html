Maple Banana Nut Muffins

These maple banana nut muffins are moist and tasty, with a hard-to-beat combination of banana, maple, and walnuts. Serve them warm with maple butter for extra richness.

Home

Ingredients

Muffins

  • 2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 3 medium ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/2 cup pure maple syrup
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/3 cup salted butter, melted
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts

Topping

  • 1/4 cup maple sugar or packed brown sugar
  • 3 tablespoons finely chopped walnuts
  • 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 3 tablespoons salted butter, cut into 4 slices

directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C). Lightly grease or line 16 to 18 2 1/2-inch muffin cups with paper liners. Coat liners with nonstick cooking spray.
  2. Whisk flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt together in a medium bowl; set aside.
  3. Beat bananas, maple syrup, sour cream, melted butter, egg, and vanilla with an electric mixer in a large bowl until combined.
  4. Add flour mixture to the banana mixture; stir until just combined. Stir walnuts into the mixture.
  5. Fill prepared muffin cups 3/4 full with batter. A heaping #20 (3 tablespoon) scoop is helpful for this
  6. For topping, combine maple sugar, walnuts, flour, and cinnamon in a medium bowl. Use a fork to mix in sliced butter until mixture is crumbly; sprinkle topping evenly over muffin batter.
  7. Bake for 5 minutes. Reduce oven temperature to 375 degrees F (90 degrees C). Bake until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean, about 10 minutes.
  8. Cool muffins on a wire rack for 10 minutes before removing from the pan.
